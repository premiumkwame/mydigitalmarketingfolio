BARBERING & GROOMING · GHANA
THE PROJECT

A digital home for the brand.

The live website introduces Fade Flex Studios as a premium barbering studio in Accra. Its challenge is to communicate the studio's personality and service experience while making it easy for visitors to learn about the business and take the next step.

The website experience brings together brand messaging, founder information, service highlights, location and contact details, and calls to book a session.

01 / PROJECT FOCUS

Make the brand experience clear and memorable.

This project is presented as a website design and development project. Its current portfolio story focuses on the live website and the customer-facing experience visible on the site.

02 / WEBSITE EXPERIENCE
01

Brand introduction

A clear opening message positions the studio around premium barbering, style and precision.

02

Founder story

An about section introduces the founder and communicates the values behind the studio.

03

Service experience

The experience section explains the studio's approach to craft, products and customer care.

04

Booking & contact

Calls to book, contact information, location and opening hours help visitors plan a visit.

A live website, ready for its next phase.
